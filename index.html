Data Protection Standards and Governance
Version 2.4 - Legally Binding
1. Data Controller and Educational Jurisdiction
LearnAI operates as a secure data processor for accredited educational institutions. All student educational records are processed in strict compliance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA, 20 U.S.C. 1232g), India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP), and European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation EU 2016/679).
2. Zero-PII Ingestion and Architectural Anonymization
The platform enforces strict zero-PII boundaries:
  • Student identifying data (real names, government student IDs, institutional email addresses) are never transmitted to external computational APIs.
  • All diagnostic prompts and code traces are scrubbed locally using in-memory cryptographic pseudonymization prior to telemetry processing.
  • Code execution and step-tracing run entirely client-side within the student browser sandbox.
3. Ephemeral Telemetry and Zero Model Training
Student assignment submissions, source code, and diagnostic quiz responses are never used to train public or proprietary machine learning models. Instructional telemetry is retained solely within the institutional database and purged in accordance with institutional data retention schedules.
4. Rights of Access, Portability, and Erasure
Enrolled students and faculty maintain the right to inspect all diagnostic telemetry recorded under their institutional identifier, export submitted work in standard text formats, and request permanent deletion of historical assessment attempts upon course completion.
